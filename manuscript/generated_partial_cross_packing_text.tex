Seven operating conditions have valid finite-volume solutions on both the seed101 and seed202 pebble arrangements. Across these paired cases, the mean absolute relative changes in outlet temperature and maximum solid temperature are 0.524\% and 0.087\%, with maxima of 0.661\% and 0.305\%, respectively. The seed202 pressure drop is higher in every paired condition, by 14.67--17.99\% (mean absolute change 16.47\%). Thus, for the two arrangements and seven common conditions tested here, the integral thermal response is substantially less sensitive to the packing realization than the hydraulic resistance. This comparison is not a complete nine-condition packing study: the two seed202 cases at $u=\SI{0.25}{m.s^{-1}}$ and $T_{\mathrm{in}}=\SI{900}{K}$ are excluded because their nonlinear pressure iterates exceed the declared helium viscosity-table range, and no extrapolated material property is introduced.
