The 60 local-crop fields span $Re_{p,\mathrm{ax}}=0.222$--$4.705$ and $Pr=0.662$--$0.669$. Of the 12 cases for which $\overline{T}_s>\overline{T}_f$ and the declared $Re_p<1.8$ range both hold, 2 (16.7\%) lie within $\pm30\%$ of the published whole-bed P417 Nusselt correlation; the largest absolute difference is 89.3\%. The remaining 30 conditions have $\overline{T}_s\leq\overline{T}_f$ in the wall-adjacent crop and are not forced into the positive phase-temperature-difference definition of P419. This difference from the source paper's whole-bed statistic is not removed by refitting: the present domain is a smaller wall-adjacent crop, and the source paper does not publish the spatial averaging operation used for $Re_{p,\mathrm{AVE}}$ and $Pr_{\mathrm{AVE}}$. The correlation is therefore retained as an aggregate literature reference rather than a local-field label or a neural-network loss. Direct integration of the finite-volume fluid--solid interface gives $Nu_{sf}=4.033$--$9.012$; its heat-flow direction agrees with $\overline{T}_s-\overline{T}_f$ in 60 of 60 cases. The maximum solid energy-partition difference is 0.054\% of generated power. This resolved interface quantity, rather than the aggregate P419 generated-power definition, supplies the local finite-volume heat-transfer target used by the neural models.
Using the resolved crop porosity and length together with the superficial velocity through the complete inlet section, the P420/P421 modified pressure relation differs from the resolved pressure drop by 1.80\% at the median and 19.7\% at most; 45 of 60 cases also lie inside the source full-domain P422 value. The published inlet-channel volume flow is preserved by area-correcting the velocity on the open fluid part of the cropped inlet; that pore-opening velocity is not inserted directly into the packed-bed relation.
