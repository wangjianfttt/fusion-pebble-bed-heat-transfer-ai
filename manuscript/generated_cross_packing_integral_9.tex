All nine tested operating conditions have valid finite-volume solutions on both the seed101 and seed202 pebble arrangements. Across these paired cases, the mean absolute relative changes in outlet temperature and maximum solid temperature are 0.533\% and 0.129\%, with maxima of 0.661\% and 0.305\%, respectively. The seed202 pressure drop is higher in every paired condition, by 14.67--17.99\% (mean absolute change 16.10\%). Thus, for the two arrangements and 9 common conditions tested here, the integral thermal response is substantially less sensitive to the packing realization than the hydraulic resistance. The comparison therefore covers the eight corners and the interior point of the independent-packing test matrix. It quantifies packing sensitivity of integral thermal and hydraulic responses; it does not replace the separate frozen-model field prediction test.
